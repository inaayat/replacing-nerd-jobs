ⓘ Tournament Guide

Who Qualifies

Top 2 from each group (24 teams) automatically advance to the Round of 32
Best 8 third-place teams across all 12 groups also advance (ranked by points → GD → goals scored)
4th place teams and the worst 4 third-place teams are eliminated

⚖️ Group Stage Tiebreakers

When teams are equal on points, FIFA separates them in this order:

1

Head-to-head points

Points in matches only between the tied teams

2

Head-to-head goal difference

GD only in matches between tied teams

3

Head-to-head goals scored

Total goals in matches between tied teams

4

Overall goal difference

GD across all group stage matches

5

Overall goals scored

Total goals in all group matches

6

Fair play points

Yellow=−1 · Direct red=−3 · YR=−4

7

Drawing of lots

Random FIFA draw if all criteria are equal
